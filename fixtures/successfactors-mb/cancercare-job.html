Staff Pharmacist - Repost

CancerCare Manitoba is a provincial agency which provides clinical and academic services and leadership for cancer control and the treatment of blood disorders. Our shared vision of a world free of cancer drives every member of our team. CancerCare Manitoba is the perfect place to thrive if you're seeking a fulfilling career in an organization that is committed to its employees' well-being and professional development.

 

Competition # 288-26-7144001/764009695

Posting End Date: Open Until Filled

City:  Winnipeg

Work Location:  MacCharles

Department:  Pharmacy

Union: Non-Union

Hiring Status: Term- FT, 2 year Term

Reason for Term: Other Leave

Anticipated Start - End Date: ASAP - September 15, 2028

FTE: 1.0

Anticipated Shift:  Days/Modified Days - Weekends Required, On-Call Required

Daily Hours Worked:  7.75

Annual Base Hours:  2015

Salary:  $60.744 $62.625, $64.584, $66.615, $68.745, $70.963

 

 

 

Job Summary:

 The Pharmacy at CancerCare Manitoba is a dynamic environment suited to a Pharmacist with a desire to expand their knowledge base in Oncology and one who possesses a genuine concern for patient care. Pharmacy services include I.V. antineoplastic admixture, investigational drug studies, provision of drug information, and patient teaching at one of the six CancerCare Manitoba sites.  Note: The successful candidate will be working with hazardous materials on a daily basis.  Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and training are provided.  Primary working hours are Monday to Friday day shifts. 

 

Qualifications:

 

Required

  • Graduate of Pharmacy from a recognized University
  • Licensed to practice in Manitoba
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, organizational and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work both independently and as a member of a multi-disciplinary team
  • Accuracy and attentive to details
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Outlook/Word/Excel
  • Knowledge of current pharmacy computer systems and software
  • Satisfactory employment record
  • Must be able to legally work in Canada

 

Preferred

  • Three (3) years’ experience as a Staff Pharmacist
  • Experience in an institutional pharmacy
  • Experience with oncology pharmacy

 

Asset

  • Commitment to ongoing professional development
  • Leadership and self-development
  • Ability to speak French

 

Interested and qualified applicants are invited to submit a detailed and current resume, plus a cover letter demonstrating how you meet the above qualifications and the above competition number.

 

This position is subject to a Criminal Record Check, including Vulnerable Sector Search, an Adult Abuse Registry Check and a Child Abuse Registry Check. The successful candidate will be responsible for any service charges incurred.

 

CancerCare Manitoba is committed to developing inclusive, barrier-free recruitment processes and work environment.  If you are contacted by CancerCare Manitoba regarding a job opportunity or testing, please advise if accommodation measures must be taken to enable you to apply and be assessed in a fair and equitable manner.

 

This information is available in alternative formats on request. Please contact CCMBAskHR@cancercare.mb.ca 

 

We thank all that apply and advise that only those applicants selected for further consideration will be contacted.
